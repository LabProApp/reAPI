Grievance Redressal

This Grievance Redressal Mechanism (“Mechanism”) outlines the structured process by which Users (“You”, “User”) may submit complaints, disputes, or concerns arising from the use of the real estate platform (“Platform”). The Platform is committed to addressing grievances in a transparent, systematic, and time-bound manner in accordance with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

1. Definitions

For the purposes of this Mechanism:

  • “Grievance” means any complaint, concern, dispute, or objection submitted by a User regarding property listings, documentation services, loan facilitation, Platform features, or third-party interactions facilitated through the Platform.
  • “Grievance Officer” refers to the designated individual responsible for receiving, acknowledging, reviewing, and resolving User grievances as required under applicable law.
  • “User” includes any individual or entity accessing, visiting, or utilizing the Platform and its Services.

2. Appointment of Grievance Officer

In compliance with applicable laws and internal governance standards, the Platform appoints a dedicated Grievance Officer to handle and oversee the resolution of grievances. The Officer shall act as the primary point of contact for all User complaints and shall ensure fair, impartial, and timely resolution.

3. Contact Details of Grievance Officer

Users may submit grievances through any of the following channels:

4. Submission of Grievances

Users may lodge grievances through:

  • Email submissions to the official grievance address containing a detailed description of the issue, supporting documents, screenshots, and relevant account or listing details.
  • Written letters submitted to the registered office address, clearly marked “Attention: Grievance Officer”.
  • Online submission forms (if available) via the Platform’s dedicated grievance page.

5. Required Information for Processing

To enable prompt and efficient resolution, the User shall provide:

  • Full name, registered email address, and phone number associated with the User’s Platform account.
  • Detailed description of the grievance, including dates, parties involved, and nature of the issue.
  • Supporting documentation or evidence such as screenshots, invoices, communication logs, or uploaded documents in question.
  • Transaction identifiers related to property listings, legal documentation requests, or loan facilitation submissions.
  • Any prior communication with customer support relevant to the grievance.

6. Acknowledgment of Grievances

Upon receipt of a grievance, the Platform shall issue an acknowledgment to the User within 48 (forty-eight) working hours. The acknowledgment shall include:

  • A unique Grievance Reference Number for tracking the complaint.
  • Name and contact details of the assigned Grievance Officer or support representative.
  • An indicative timeline for resolution.

7. Review and Investigation

The Grievance Officer shall:

  • Conduct a preliminary assessment of the grievance to determine its validity, severity, and required documentation for a thorough review.
  • Initiate internal investigation processes, including review of Platform logs, correspondence records, property documentation submissions, and relevant transaction histories.
  • Engage with third-party service providers when applicable, clarifying that the Platform does not assume liability for third-party actions.
  • Request supplementary information from the User if required to make an informed determination.

8. Resolution Timeline

The Platform aims to resolve grievances within 15 (fifteen) working days from the date of acknowledgment. If additional time is required due to complexity, User inaction, or third-party dependencies, the User shall be informed of an updated timeline in writing.

9. Categories of Grievances

Grievances may relate to, but are not limited to:

  • Incorrect, misleading, or fraudulent property listings uploaded by Users, brokers, or third parties.
  • Disputes regarding legal documentation services facilitated through third-party vendors.
  • Loan facilitation concerns involving Lending Partner responsiveness, delays, or discrepancies.
  • Technical issues affecting Platform access, data visibility, profile usage, or transactions.
  • Unauthorized or suspicious account activity detected by the User.
  • Content moderation issues relating to removal or retention of listings.

10. Non-Compliance, Rejection, or Closure of Grievances

A grievance may be closed without resolution if:

  • The User fails to provide required information or documents despite repeated reminders.
  • The grievance pertains to services directly controlled by third-party vendors or Lending Partners for which the Platform bears no contractual responsibility.
  • The complaint is found to be frivolous, repetitive, malicious, or beyond the Platform’s operational scope.
  • The issue has already been addressed and a response has been provided earlier.

11. No Guarantee of Outcome

While the Platform endeavors to address grievances fairly, the User acknowledges that the Platform:

  • Does not guarantee reversal, monetary compensation, legal remedy, or punitive action against any third-party service provider.
  • Is not responsible for delays, negligence, inaccuracies, or decisions made by property owners, legal vendors, or Lending Partners.
  • Does not intervene in contractual, transactional, or financial disputes between the User and third parties.

12. Escalation Matrix

If the User believes that the grievance has not been satisfactorily addressed, the User may escalate the matter to:

  • Senior Grievance Officer (contact details available upon written request).
  • Regulatory or statutory authorities as permitted under applicable laws, after exhausting the Platform’s internal grievance process.

13. Record Maintenance

The Platform shall maintain detailed records of all grievances, acknowledgments, correspondences, supporting materials, investigation notes, and resolution outcomes for a minimum period of 180 (one hundred eighty) days or as mandated under applicable laws.

14. Amendments to Grievance Mechanism

The Platform reserves the right to revise, modify, or update this Mechanism at any time without prior notice. Continued use of the Platform following any amendment shall constitute deemed acceptance of the revised Mechanism.
